# Privacy Policy – Nyxen Player

**Last Updated: November 10, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Nyxen Player.

Nyxen Player is a music player application designed to provide users with a modern and personalized music listening experience.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use Nyxen Player.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Nyxen Player may process the following information:

- **Google Account Information:** When you sign in with Google, we may receive your name, email address, and profile picture.
- **Local Music Files:** The application may access audio files stored on your device to provide music playback.
- **App Preferences:** Settings such as themes, playlists, playback preferences, and equalizer configurations may be stored locally.
- **Technical Information:** Basic technical information may be processed to maintain application functionality and troubleshoot errors.

We do not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary for the application's functionality.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information processed by Nyxen Player may be used to:

- Authenticate users through Google Sign-In.
- Provide account-related features.
- Organize and play local music files.
- Save user preferences and application settings.
- Improve application stability and performance.
- Troubleshoot technical problems.

## 4. Google Sign-In

Nyxen Player may offer Google Sign-In as an authentication method.

When you choose to sign in, Google may share certain account information with the application, including your name, email address, and profile picture.

Google account information is used only for the purposes disclosed when you authorize access.

Nyxen Player does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or other Google services unless explicitly disclosed and authorized.

Your use of Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Music and Third-Party Services

Nyxen Player may support local music playback and integrations with third-party music platforms.

If third-party services such as Spotify or SoundCloud are integrated, their respective privacy policies and terms of service apply to your use of those services.

Nyxen Player does not claim ownership of music or other content provided by third-party platforms.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

Application preferences and other supported information may be stored locally on your device or processed by external services where required for application functionality.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party providers when necessary to provide features requested by users, such as Google authentication.

We do not share Google user data with third parties for advertising purposes.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Nyxen Player is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

## 9. Your Privacy Choices

You may:

- Stop using Google Sign-In.
- Revoke Nyxen Player's access through your Google Account settings.
- Remove locally stored application data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling it.
- Stop using the application at any time.

Deleting local application data does not necessarily delete information held by third-party services.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in application features, data practices, or legal requirements.
